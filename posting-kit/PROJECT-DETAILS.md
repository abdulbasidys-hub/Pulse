# Project details

**Name:** PULSE

**Credit:** A concept project by Nightfall.

**Tagline:** Your work is global. Your payments should be too.

**One-line description:** A fictional stablecoin payments workspace for freelancers and digital businesses receiving and tracking global payments.

## Capabilities represented

- Create and share payment links.
- Receive supported stablecoins.
- Track incoming-payment status.
- View transaction activity in one dashboard.

## What was built

A responsive landing page, desktop/mobile product previews, a front-end payment-link demo, a banner, and a coordinated posting asset kit. The site and exported product artwork share canonical desktop/mobile SVG masters and the same illustrative data.

**Stack:** React, JavaScript, Vite, CSS. Poppins is bundled locally.

## Product status

PULSE is fictional. All names, balances and transaction statuses are illustrative. No real accounts, wallet connections, custody, withdrawals, conversion, or payment processing are implemented. Describe this work as a product design and front-end portfolio concept.
